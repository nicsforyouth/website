## Welcome to PARSE IT!

This workshop will take a dive into strings—one of the most important datatypes in all of programming.

This entire workshop has one continuous goal:

```py
# You type:

12 * (5 + 4) - 4

# Your program understands and outputs:

92
```

That’s it. That’s what you’ll do in this course. In the process, you’ll learn what strings are to a computer, how to process it and how to parse it logically.

The workshop is categorized into **11 modules** over **2 days**

## Day 1

Introduction to Strings

Breaking Things Apart

Tokens: Giving Meaning to Pieces

**Mini Challenge: Build a Tokenizer**

## Day 2

The BODMAS rule

Rules, programmatically

Building the Parser

Breaking Your Parser

**Ship It!**
