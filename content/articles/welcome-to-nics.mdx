---

## Why We Started NICS

Many students are interested in technology but don't know where to begin.
Some schools have active computer clubs, while others have none at all. It is easy to get lost hunting for opportunities,
especially as a young teenager buried in high-school assignments.

And well... we wanted to change that. Instead of waiting for opportunities to appear, we decided to create them.

By bringing together motivated students from across Nepal, we hope to create an environment where learning is accessible.
And through active events, we aim to encourage collaboration on projects, like in the real world.

---

## Our Mission

Our mission is to empower the next generation of Nepali innovators by providing an inclusive community focused on technology, learning, and innovation.
Rather than competing against one another, we believe students grow faster when they learn together, share knowledge, and support each other's ideas.


---

## Our Vision

We envision NICS as a prominent platform for young high-schoolers to pursue their interests in computer science and related fields.
We believe that with support from our members, we can help young minds develop their skills and show their talents even before they become a undergrad scholar.

In the long run, we hope NICS becomes a platform that connects students with mentors, competitions, research opportunities, and one another.

---

## Our Objectives

NICS aims to:

- Build an active community of technology enthusiasts across Nepal.
- Make learning computer science more accessible for beginners.
- Encourage students to build projects and work in team.
- Organize workshops, coding sessions, and educational events.
- Publish articles and educational resources written for, and by students.
- Promote collaboration between schools and student communities.
- Prepare students for competitions, hackathons, and project showcases.
- Foster leadership, communication, and teamwork alongside technical skills.

No matter your current skill level, there's always something new to explore.

---

## Who Can Join?

NICS primarily welcomes **high school students** from across Nepal.

You don't need to be an expert programmer. You don't need awards. 

You don't even need prior experience.

If you're curious, willing to learn, and excited about technology, you're already the kind of person we're looking for.

<Callout type="info">

Experienced students are equally welcome to contribute, mentor others, and help strengthen the community.
For such memberships, please apply via the same [application form](https://nicsforyouth.vercel.app/join) and mention your
qualifications & projects under the "interests" section.

</Callout>
