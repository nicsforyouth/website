…Do I even need to yap about it? Anybody who has passed middle-school knows about the BODMAS (sometimes called PEMDAS) rule. But since we are doing this, lemme just revise it to you real quick:

## BODMAS

BODMAS stands for:

Brackets —> Orders —> Division —> Multiplication —> Addition —> Subtraction

But that’s not *entirely* true. Division and multiplication have the same precedence, as well as addition and subtraction. The only catch is that **everything is evaluated from left to right.** So if subtraction comes before addition, we’ll subtract first. Same for multiplication vs. divison.

- Orders are exponents (and roots), which we won’t get into for now :)

So BODMAS—summarized is basically: **Multiplication and division happen before addition and subtraction.** That is all we need for our parser!
